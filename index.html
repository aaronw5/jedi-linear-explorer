What is the tiny trigger tagger doing?

Interpretability explorer for the quantized JEDI-linear jet tagger (3 inputs per particle: pT, Δη, Δφ), classes g, q, W, Z, t.

Every neuron of the network written as an exact formula of the inputs and matched to jet physics; transcoders and sparse autoencoders; decision trees distilled from the network, clickable node by node. All numbers are on 50,000 held-out jets.
